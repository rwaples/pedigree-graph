# pedigree-graph 0.7.1 to 0.8.0: measured consumer migration

**Closed archive.** This directory is the record of the 0.8.0 to 0.10.0
migration: the 0.7.1 to 0.8.0 comparison below and the per-slice gate records
under `gate/` (9a to 16e). Nothing new is written here; release gates since
then go to `docs/release-gates/<stage>/`. The notes use the tool names of
their time:

Slice 8 of the pedigree-graph 0.8.0 plan migrated simACE, fitACE,
fitACE_epimight, and pedsum. 0.8 corrects kinship values and classifies each
pair under one closest category, so byte parity was never the gate. This
directory holds the rerunnable comparison instead.

- `baseline/` was captured under the PyPI 0.7.1 wheel before any consumer edit.
- `migrated/` was captured under the 0.8 source checkout after all four
  consumer commits.
- `report.md` is every leaf that differs, generated by
  `tools/pg08_migration_diff.py compare`.

Regenerate either side with the tool's `snapshot` subcommand after rebuilding
the smoke scenario (`results/test/small_test`, simACE targets plus the fitACE
`grm`, `pcgc`, `iter_reml_fp{32,64}`, `pafgrs`, and `exports` targets); the
tool's docstring has the commands (the tool is removed; see the table above).

## What moved, and why

Smoke scenario: N=1000, G_ped=6, three reps, `max_degree=3`, GRM threshold
0.001. Numbers below are rep1 unless stated; the other reps are in `report.md`.

**Closest-category fold (all consumers).** 0.7.1 could list a pair under two
codes; 0.8 reports it once under the lowest degree. On the smoke pedigree the
only affected code is half-avuncular: `HAv` 6380 to 6372 pairs in the analysis
sample, 11141 to 11125 in the full pedigree, and the pair export loses the same
16 rows (103659 to 103643). `MZ`, `MO`, `FO`, `FS`, `MHS`, `PHS`, `GP`, `Av`,
`GAv`, and `1C` are unchanged. Codes above the requested degree serialize as
`null` ("not computed") where 0.7.1 wrote `0`.

**Validation correlations and quality checks: unchanged.** Every liability
correlation, half-sib count, heritability estimate, and quality-check verdict
agrees to floating-point noise (max absolute difference 3e-8 on a tetrachoric,
1e-15 elsewhere). The canonical re-sort of pair blocks did not move the
subsampled correlations at this size.

**Effective size.** Ne values agree on the estimators that ran under both
versions; the record shape changes. Arrays are indexed by observed generation
label (`generations`, `parent_generations`, `transition_from`/`transition_to`),
the transition-rate estimators carry `len(generations) - 1` entries in
`ne_per_gen`, and the unrequested `ne_coancestry` slot is
`{reason: not_requested, code: null, fields: {}}` with no `ne`.
`ne_long_term_contributions` reported no estimate under both versions.

**Approximate GRM values (fitACE, iter-REML, PCGC).** `write_pedigree` with
`min_kinship=0.001` now writes exact values on the propagation candidate
support instead of propagation-pruned values. The support did not change on
the smoke pedigree (identical `A.grm.sp.bin` sizes), every value did (different
hashes). Downstream, iter-REML and PCGC variance components move in the third
to fourth decimal: rep3 iter-REML A from 0.4620 to 0.4626, C from 0.0181 to
0.0179; rep3 PCGC A from 0.4339 to 0.4337. PA-FGRS relative sets now come from
`relationship_kinship_matrix(max_degree=2)` (category support) rather than a
threshold: the degree-2 support on the full rep1 pedigree drops from 53256 to
52382 upper-triangle entries, and PA-FGRS `r` moves by at most 0.005 (mean
-0.004 in rep1) with AUC within 0.004.

**Inbreeding (fitACE export, pedsum).** `inbreeding()` is MZ-aware
genome-node Meuwissen-Luo where 0.7.1 was MZ-naive. The export gains five rows
in rep1 (309 to 314 individuals with F > 0), four in rep2, two in rep3; the
largest single change is 0.125 for one rep2 individual.

**Library-level checks on the full rep1 pedigree (6000 rows).** The
approximate matrix at 0.001 now matches the complete matrix exactly on its
support (max difference 0 versus 0.00049 under 0.7.1). `descendant_path_counts`
is int64. The fold-aware estimator declares six exact codes (`MZ`, `MO`, `FO`,
`FS`, `MHS`, `PHS`); 0.7.1 declared ten.

**pedsum.** On `example_pedigree.tsv` with `--per-individual-pairs` the fold
moves four counts: `1C1R` 1117 to 856, `1C2R` 774 to 608, `HGAv` 517 to 490,
`HGGAv` 303 to 256. On a parent-offspring-incest fixture the fold also moves
`PHS` 5 to 3, `GP` 11 to 9, `HAv` 6 to 2, `H1C` 6 to 0, `Av` 3 to 1. Effective
size records gain their depth labels and `final_depth`; the extra YAML grows
accordingly (507 to 577 lines on the example). pedsum's own `CHANGELOG.md`
records the user-facing list.

**random_30k (library only).** See the section below; this case is the plan's
30k integration gate for the kinship paths.

## random_30k

The parity fixture (30300 rows, seed 30000). The single-process 0.8 run was
killed by the session's memory guard, so the 0.8 side ran one stage per
process (`migrated/library.json` records wall time and peak RSS per stage).

- Pairs at degree 3: only `GGP` moves under the fold, 149813 to 149807; the
  other eleven populated codes are identical. `estimate_relationship_counts`
  declares six exact codes.
- Degree-2 kinship support: 251407 upper-triangle entries under the 0.7.1
  threshold, 238796 under `relationship_kinship_matrix(max_degree=2)`, in
  45 s and 773 MiB.
- Approximate matrix at 0.001: identical support (26924109 upper-triangle
  entries) with exact instead of pruned values, 76 s and 6.9 GiB peak, which
  matches pedigree-graph's `benchmarks/matrix_exactification.md`. 0.7.1's
  pruned values were off by up to 0.0015 on that support.
- Inbreeding: 6669 individuals with F > 0 under both; the MZ-aware sum rises
  from 12.961 to 13.128 and the maximum from 0.12646 to 0.12720.
- `descendant_path_counts` is int64 with the same values.
- The 134M-entry complete matrix was not rebuilt under 0.8 on this box; the
  approximate stage already runs the complete retiring DP pass.

Consumer consequence worth carrying into slice 9's performance gate: fitACE's
`write_pedigree(min_kinship=0.001)` now costs the complete DP pass (about
7 GiB at 30k rows) where 0.7.1's pruned pass cost about 2.4 GiB. The
`baseline100K` scenarios run this path at 100k rows and have not been
re-measured.
